# QAHAF client demo

Open the [QAHAF demo](https://swapnilmahfuz.github.io/fashion-pos-demo/) and keep it in one tab for the walkthrough. Use sample names and numbers. Refreshing the browser starts a fresh session.

## Receive and sell a new style

1. As Owner, select **New Market**. In **Contacts → Suppliers**, add a sample supplier. In **Customer Groups**, add Regular with a 10% discount. Add a sample customer and assign the group. Enable SMS or WhatsApp preferences only for demonstration contacts intended for those previews.
2. Select **Combined**. In **Products & Inventory**, add a linen shirt with sizes M and L and colours Black and Navy. Generate the four variants. Set the unit buying price to ৳400 and selling price to ৳1,000. Save with availability at both branches.
3. Return to **New Market**. In **Purchases**, receive six units of the Black M variant and two units of a second variant at ৳400 each. Enter ৳600 paid into the purchase, leaving ৳2,600 due. In **Products & Inventory**, print or preview the Black M Code 128 label.
4. In **POS & Sales**, scan the barcode twice. Enter a ৳100 flat invoice discount, choose the Regular customer in checkout, and collect ৳1,700 to the default Cash Box. The group reduces each ৳1,000 unit by ৳100 before the invoice discount. The walk-in option also remains available.
5. From **Sales History**, return one sellable unit and refund ৳850. In **Stock Transfers**, dispatch two units from New Market. Switch to **Dakbangla** and receive them. The Black M balance is now three units in New Market and two in Dakbangla at ৳400 each.

## Reconcile and export

6. Record a ৳100 unpaid delivery expense in New Market. Settle it from Cash Box in **Expenses**. **Funds & Accounts** should show ৳150 left in New Market Cash Box for these transactions. The supplier balance is ৳2,600.
7. Open **Reports & Analytics → Profit/Loss**. Choose **Today**, **Combined** and **Linked session activity only**. With the default 0% demo VAT, net sales are ৳850, COGS ৳400, gross profit ৳450 and operating profit ৳350. The inventory report shows both branch balances. Download XLSX, CSV and PDF.
8. Open **Marketing → Compose**. Select the opted-in sample customer, enter a message, and review segment count and estimated cost. Save a draft or simulate it and inspect **SMS History**. The simulated entry shows **Not sent**. WhatsApp opens a prefilled draft only when you choose its link.

## Verify branch settings

9. In **New Market → Settings → Invoices & Receipts**, set prefix NM, a sample header, and the 80 mm layout. Open **Preview and print**. Switch to Dakbangla to inspect its settings, then reopen the earlier sale to see its original receipt snapshot.
10. In **Settings → Security**, preview Manager access. The Manager sees their assigned branch and its operational reports. The Owner can return to Combined financial analytics through the role selector.
